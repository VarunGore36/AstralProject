What is Astral

Most backtests answer the wrong question.

They ask would this idea have made money — on data nobody can inspect, with fills nobody can verify, under assumptions nobody wrote down. A quoted spread is treated as an executable opportunity. A paper result is presented as performance.

Astral asks a smaller, harder question instead: can we measure whether an apparent opportunity is actually executable — after fees, slippage, latency, liquidity, market impact, execution constraints, realistic fills and capital constraints?

It answers with a continuously-collected, provenance-tracked market-data corpus, a deterministic replay engine, and an honest execution-cost harness. Every result carries its dataset hash, its assumptions, and a reproduction command. A rigorous “this has no edge” is a successful result here.

Architecture

A pipeline you can audit at every joint.

Raw venue bytes in. Reproducible claims out. Nothing in between is a black box.

Lossless capture

Raw frames written to compressed, content-addressed chunks with capture timestamps and a SHA-256 integrity index. Drops and gaps are recorded as data, never absorbed silently.

Checked reconstruction

L2 books rebuilt from diffs on a snapshot bootstrap, with venue update-ID continuity enforced. A broken book rejects events until a new snapshot — it never silently resumes.

Deterministic replay

Same capture in, byte-identical results out, on any machine. The precondition for every claim the project will ever make.

Open source

The engine is open. Run it yourself.

Apache-2.0. The full research loop runs locally on your own machine — record, verify, reconstruct, report. Everything essential is in the open.

Principles

The epistemics are the product.

Reproducibility

Same inputs, same code, same result — on any machine. Every claim carries a reproduction command.

Realistic execution

No fill without a trade print through your price. No quoted spread presented as an opportunity.

Provenance

Every byte traced to its venue, its capture, its schema version. Quality tiers, not blind trust.

No fake profitability

Backtests are labelled backtests. Paper is not live. Optimistic models are marked unrealistic in the output itself.

Transparent assumptions

Fees, slippage, latency, capital, execution model — on the chart, not in a footnote.

Negative results count

“No edge” is a first-class, publishable finding. Refutation is the mechanism that makes positive claims worth trusting.

Roadmap

Gates with kill criteria, not a feature wishlist.

Each stage must earn the next one with evidence. If a gate fails, the project stops or pivots — that outcome is designed in.

  1. 01

    Capture & reconstruct

    In progress

    Lossless recording, verified book reconstruction, offline audit. Judged by a 72-hour soak with zero unexplained loss.

  2. 02

    Does it work

    Planned

    Shadow mode measures predicted fills against what was actually tradable. Dies if model error exceeds ~10 bps on liquid pairs.

  3. 03

    Useful research

    Planned

    One honest published research note. Dies if three months of real use produces nothing substantive.

  4. 04

    Do others use it

    Planned

    External users running the recorder, external reproductions of published results. Dies quietly if nobody comes.

  5. 05

    Beyond

    Undefined

    Further stages are defined from what Gate 4 teaches, not before. The public roadmap ends where the evidence ends.
