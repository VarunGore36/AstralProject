Research note 006: timing the carry by premium percentile improves drift and dies on fees

Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: two pinned windows, hourly bars, stated assumptions. Answers the timing question both carry notes deferred: enter only when the perp premium sits at/above its trailing 75th percentile, exit below the trailing median — trailing-only percentiles (720h minimum warmup, no look-ahead), same legs, fees, and fills as notes 004/005 so only the entry rule changed.

Question

Notes 004/005 found carry surviving costs only when funding runs hot. Can selectivity buy back the dead quarters — harvest the rich-premium hours, sit out the rest?

Dataset and method

  • The pinned slices behind notes 004 (Q1 2024) and 005 (Q4 2022): 549 funding payments, 4,392 hourly bars per leg, 0 misaligned hours.
  • The nine-month hole between the quarters (2023-01..09, no pinned data) splits the study into two contiguous blocks; no state crosses it — percentiles, warmup, and positions all reset per block. A rule that traded across unobserved months would be measuring its own absence.
  • Baseline rerun per block: unconditional hold over the same post-warmup windows (open + close per block = $40 in fees, not $20 — two blocks).
  • Timed rule: hysteresis band (enter ≥ trailing p75, exit < trailing p50), funding credited when open at the payment timestamp, fills at closes.

Results

Measure Unconditional baseline Timed (p75/p50)
Round trips (fills) 2 opens + 2 closes ($40) ~163 (326 fills, $3,260)
Bars open all 2,952 1,841 (62%)
Funding collected $472.52 $454.89 (96% of baseline)
Basis drift PnL −$52.59 +$255.45
Net +$379.93 −$2,549.66

Reading

Two facts pulling opposite ways, and the fees win by an order of magnitude. First, selectivity does what it promises on market terms: it collects 96% of the funding in 62% of the time and turns basis drift from −$53 into +$255 — rich-premium hours really are the hours worth holding. Second, it pays 163 round trips for the privilege: $3,260 in fees against $455 of harvested funding, a round trip every ~18 hours at $20 a turn. The unconditional hold wins not by being smarter but by barely trading. The honest version of "timing works": timing the premium is real edge on drift and negative edge after turnover, at $10k size and 5 bps a fill.

What would strengthen or overturn this: wider hysteresis (fewer turns — done as note 007, same script, two flags); maker instead of taker fills where the book supports it; a full consecutive year so no block splitting is needed; and the live-captured version, where fills stop being assumed and start being simulated.

Reproduce

python3 research/scripts/carry_006.py --data-dir /tmp/carry006

Eighteen archive SHA-256 hashes pinned in the script (the nine behind each of notes 004/005, re-enforced on every download), refused on any other bytes.
