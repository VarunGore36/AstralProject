Research note 007: wider bands bleed slower, and still lose to doing nothing

Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: same two windows, five hysteresis bands, hourly bars, stated assumptions. The follow-up note 006 promised ("wider hysteresis — the obvious next run, same script, two constants"): enter at p75/p90, exit at p50/p25/p10, everything else identical — same legs, fees, fills, trailing-only percentiles, per-block discipline.

Question

Note 006 found selectivity improving drift and dying on turnover. Is turnover a tunable cost — do wider bands rescue the timed rule?

Method

research/scripts/carry_006.py grew --enter-pct/--exit-pct flags (defaults reproduce note 006 exactly: net −$2,549.66, verified). Five bands run against the same 549 payments and 4,392 hourly bars. All five results are reported below — selecting only the best would be the very p-hacking this series exists to refuse.

Reading

The gradient is the finding, not any single row. Every column moves monotonically with band width: fewer turns, less fee bleed, smaller drift edge, funding share steady near 100%. Turnover is the dominant variable — at $20 a round trip, even 21 turns cost more than the entire drift edge they buy. The widest band scrapes positive (+$80.75) while still trailing the unconditional hold by $300, and that $80 sits inside the same noise as note 005's +$55: one basis point of slippage per fill erases it four times over. Selectivity is confirmed real (drift improves in every timed row) and confirmed insufficient (fees dominate in every timed row). There is no band width where both statements stop being true at once — the fee per turn is a floor, and the premium does not pay it often enough.

What would strengthen or overturn this: maker fills where the book supports them (the fee floor is the whole game — halve it and rerun the table); sizing turns by conviction instead of fixed notional; and the live-captured version, where the turns stop being assumed.

Reproduce

python3 research/scripts/carry_006.py --data-dir /tmp/carry006 --enter-pct 90 --exit-pct 10

Defaults reproduce note 006; flags walk the table. Eighteen archive SHA-256 hashes pinned, refused on any other bytes.
