Research note 011: imbalance persists, equally, in every session

Date: 2026-10-16 · Status: one week, hourly buckets, stated pairs. Answers the persistence half of note 009's deferred questions: spot taker-buy share varies by session — but does an imbalanced hour predict the next one, and does that memory itself vary by session?

Question

Note 009 found spot direction varying by session (43.9% → 37.6% taker-buy, Asia to US) while perp holds still. Two readings compete: each session has its own flow dynamics, or one tape-wide memory wearing three different levels. Lag-1 autocorrelation of hourly taker-buy share, overall and per session, separates them.

Dataset and method

  • The same seven spot aggTrades slices behind note 009 (2024-01-15..21), repinned here (identical pins, re-verified on download): 7,553,902 prints bucketed into 168 UTC hours, overall taker-buy 40.3%.
  • Pairs are consecutive hours with both hours inside one session (49 pairs each; 167 overall including boundaries). No cross-boundary pairs — midnight effects are not smuggled into any session.
  • Pearson r on shares plus r on imbalance magnitude (|share − 0.5|), each with 1/√n beside it. Correlation is descriptive here, not a model.

Results

Scope Pairs r (share) r (magnitude) ±1 SE
All consecutive hours 167 +0.36 +0.32 0.08
Asia 00–08 49 +0.34 +0.26 0.14
EU 08–16 49 +0.34 +0.31 0.14
US 16–24 49 +0.34 +0.27 0.14

Reading

Two facts that jointly retire the question. First, memory is real and modest: +0.36 overall against a 0.08 standard error — an imbalanced hour leans the same way about a third of the time beyond chance, on this week, at this scale. Second, memory does not vary by session within error: the three sessions read identically while their levels differ by six points. Direction varies by session (note 009 stands); memory does not. The tape has one persistence mechanism wearing three different levels — the simplest consistent story is a single intraday carryover process (open positions, working orders, Asia-to-US handoff) modulated by who is awake, not three different microstructures.

What would strengthen or overturn this: more weeks (49 pairs per session is thin — the error bars admit anything from +0.2 to +0.5); the same cut on perp (balanced flow may forget faster); magnitude-conditioned splits (does persistence concentrate in strongly imbalanced hours?); and the live-captured version, where hours stop being archive buckets.

Reproduce

python3 research/scripts/persist_011.py --data-dir /tmp/persist011

Seven CSV content hashes pinned in the script (the spot pins behind note 009), refused on any mismatch — verified by a from-scratch rerun with identical figures.
