Research note 012: memory lives in the imbalance, and spot remembers best

Date: 2026-10-16 · Status: one week per venue, hourly buckets, stated pairs. Closes both halves note 011 deferred: the same lag-1 cut on perp, and magnitude-conditioned splits on each tape (pairs starting from above/below-median |share − 0.5| hours).

Question

Note 011 found spot imbalance memory identical across sessions. Two refinements compete for the mechanism: balanced perp flow may forget faster, and persistence may concentrate where imbalance is strongest. Both are tested on the note-009 weeks (spot and perp aggTrades, 2024-01-15..21).

Dataset and method

  • Fourteen daily slices pinned by extracted-CSV SHA-256 in research/scripts/persist_012.py — the exact pins behind note 009 for the same files, re-verified on download here. Stdlib only, one command.
  • Lag-1 Pearson on hourly taker-buy shares overall, plus first-hour magnitude splits (median cut, ~84/83 pairs each). Standard errors beside every r; the low-n splits are read with their error bars, not past them.

Results

Scope Pairs r ±1 SE
Spot, all hours 167 +0.36 0.08
Spot, high-magnitude first 84 +0.32 0.11
Spot, low-magnitude first 83 +0.03 0.11
Perp, all hours 167 +0.12 0.08
Perp, high-magnitude first 84 +0.17 0.11
Perp, low-magnitude first 83 −0.05 0.11

Reading

Two facts that locate the memory. First, persistence concentrates entirely in imbalance: high-magnitude hours carry the full +0.32 while quiet hours read +0.03 — white noise with a standard error attached. Whatever carries over is carried by conviction hours, not by the tape in general. Second, balanced flow nearly forgets: perp manages +0.12 overall against a 0.08 error bar, a third of spot's memory with the same magnitude gradient (+0.17 vs −0.05). Combined with note 009's flat 50% perp sessions, the venue picture is now complete — perp flow is bigger, smoother, and more forgetful; spot flow is noisier, directional, and remembers where it was loud. A signal built on persistence belongs on spot, sized by conviction, and nowhere else in this dataset.

What would strengthen or overturn this: more weeks (84-pair splits are thin — the high/low gap on spot clears its bars, on perp it does not); the same cut in a stress week (does memory spike when it matters?); maker-side persistence (resting-order imbalance rather than taker prints); and the live-captured version, where hours stop being archive buckets.

Reproduce

python3 research/scripts/persist_012.py --data-dir /tmp/persist012

Fourteen CSV content hashes pinned in the script (the note-009 pins), refused on any mismatch — verified by a from-scratch rerun with identical figures.
